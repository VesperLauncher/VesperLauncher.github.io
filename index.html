Features

Built light. Runs fast.

Vesper strips the launcher down to what matters — then makes that part quick.

Unified Ecosystem

Every mod, modpack, shader, and plugin from Modrinth and CurseForge in a single search — powered by Vesper's backend databases, which keep the index current and filtered to your loader and game version.

Modrinth CurseForge Backend-indexed

Ultra-Lightweight & Smooth

A heavily optimized architecture with a deliberately small memory footprint. Vesper stays idle in the background instead of competing with the game for RAM and CPU, so your frames go where they belong.

Low memory Resource-friendly Isolated instances

13-Second Hot Launch

Relaunching a cached instance can put you back in your server or single-player world in as little as 13 seconds. Assets stay verified on disk, so Vesper skips straight to spinning up the game.

Platform

A backend doing the heavy lifting.

The client stays thin because the hard work happens upstream. Vesper relies on secure external databases and high-performance backend services to index both mod platforms, resolve compatibility, and keep your account and profiles in sync across machines — which is exactly why the desktop app can stay this small and this quick.

Unified mod index

Modrinth and CurseForge metadata, merged and kept current server-side.

Seamless mod syncing

Instances and mod sets follow you between machines.

Account services

Secure sign-in, profiles, and skin libraries.

Resolution off-device

Compatibility math runs server-side, not on your CPU.

An internet connection is required for search, syncing, and account features. Cached instances still hot launch once they're on disk.
